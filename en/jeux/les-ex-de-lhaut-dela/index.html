💗 Les EX de l’haut delà

Love is complicated!

  • Visual novel
  • Romance
  • Comedy
  • Mystery

Four girls. One boy. Far too much trouble of the heart.

Welcome to Aozora High! You play Masaru, an entirely ordinary student who was simply hoping for a quiet school year.

But a hot-tempered goth, an athlete who turns everything into a competition, a brilliant and unpredictable thinker, and a shy literature lover may have other plans for his everyday life!

Expect unexpected meetings, awkward dates, school festivals, ridiculous arguments, and perhaps even a few confessions of love.

💌 Four heroines, four stories

Get to know distinct personalities, discover their passions, and share moments that change your relationships. Each has her dreams, habits, contradictions and way of expressing her feelings. See what lies beyond first impressions!

🌸 School life full of surprises

Between classes, clubs and school events, there are plenty of chances to grow closer. Enjoy funny scenes, tender moments and situations as embarrassing as they are unforgettable.

📖 Your choices matter

Words sometimes matter more than we think. Your choices shape your relationships, events and the way your story unfolds.

What if finding love turned out to be the easy part?

Les EX de l’haut delà puts a fresh spin on dating-sim conventions in a narrative adventure full of surprises.

Some stories stay with you.
