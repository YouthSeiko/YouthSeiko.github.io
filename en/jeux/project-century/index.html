🌼 PROJECT CENTURY

Take care of me, okay?

  • Visual novel
  • Simulation
  • Management
  • Psychological drama

Your best friend is counting on you. Perhaps a little too much.

Everyday life isn’t always simple. Groceries, bills, surprises and small acts of care all demand time and money. When your best friend depends on you to move forward, every decision becomes a little more complicated.

🏡 An everyday life to organise

In Project CENTURY, you support someone who plays an important part in your life. Look after their needs, plan the days and balance different responsibilities. Some requests will be easy to meet. Others will require compromises.

💰 Every expense counts

Your budget isn’t endless. Manage your money, set priorities and decide how to meet everyday needs. A little purchase might bring joy today… but what about tomorrow?

💬 A changing relationship

Through conversations and events, discover a complex friendship shaped by attachment, habits and tension. Helping someone can be wonderful. Knowing where your responsibility ends isn’t always easy.

🌱 Finding a balance

Project CENTURY blends storytelling and management to explore human relationships, emotional dependence and personal boundaries.

Taking care of someone matters. Taking care of yourself matters too.

How much of yourself can you give?
