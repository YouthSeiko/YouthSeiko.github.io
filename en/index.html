💉 PROJECT MIRAI

First injection.

  • Vertical shooter
  • Arcade
  • Portrait mobile play
  • Anime

A tiny world. An enormous reason to fight.

Inside Mirai’s body, Akari faces fictional organic threats with her futuristic equipment. An anime-inspired world where the guardians’ light cuts through a living, unsettling landscape.

Straight into the action

Relative touch movement, automatic fire and dodging: keep your eyes on the bullets. Use a bomb or the Neuro Burst to turn the tide, then face a two-phase boss.

Rhythm, impact, personality

Organic explosions, animated scores, dynamic phonk and Akari’s Japanese lines with French subtitles accompany every run.

Where is the project now?

The current prototype focuses on Akari and a short arcade battle. Android testing is underway; an iPhone version is among the goals. No release date or public demo has been announced yet.

Next step: refining the feel of playing on a phone.

ACT 1 demo available on Microsoft Store

💗 Les EX de l’haut delà

Love is complicated!

  • Visual novel
  • Romance
  • Comedy
  • Mystery

Four girls. One boy. Far too much trouble of the heart.

Welcome to Aozora High! You play Masaru, an entirely ordinary student who was simply hoping for a quiet school year.

But a hot-tempered goth, an athlete who turns everything into a competition, a brilliant and unpredictable thinker, and a shy literature lover may have other plans for his everyday life!

Expect unexpected meetings, awkward dates, school festivals, ridiculous arguments, and perhaps even a few confessions of love.

💌 Four heroines, four stories

Get to know distinct personalities, discover their passions, and share moments that change your relationships. Each has her dreams, habits, contradictions and way of expressing her feelings. See what lies beyond first impressions!

🌸 School life full of surprises

Between classes, clubs and school events, there are plenty of chances to grow closer. Enjoy funny scenes, tender moments and situations as embarrassing as they are unforgettable.

📖 Your choices matter

Words sometimes matter more than we think. Your choices shape your relationships, events and the way your story unfolds.

What if finding love turned out to be the easy part?

Les EX de l’haut delà puts a fresh spin on dating-sim conventions in a narrative adventure full of surprises.

Some stories stay with you.

🧊 PROJECT BABYLONE

Somewhere, there is an exit.

  • Action RPG
  • Metroidvania
  • Exploration
  • Mystery

A vast complex. Thousands of cubes. One obsession: finding the exit.

You wake in an unfamiliar place. White walls, geometric rooms and cubic structures seem to stretch forever. No windows. No landscape. No clue where you are.

One thing is certain: there must be a way out.

🧩 A world built like an enormous puzzle

Explore a mysterious complex of countless connected rooms. Each passage might hide a new area, an unexpected obstacle or a secret. Learn the layout, uncover paths and gradually understand how this strange place works.

🔓 Explore, progress, return

In the spirit of metroidvanias, progress opens places that were once out of reach. Familiar areas may reveal new possibilities. Exploration, curiosity and observation will be your best allies.

🌐 The mystery outside

Why does this complex exist? How large is it? And above all… What does the world beyond its walls look like?

Project BABYLONE is an exploration adventure built around discovery and mystery.

Every room is a question. The exit is the answer.

In concept development

🌼 PROJECT CENTURY

Take care of me, okay?

  • Visual novel
  • Simulation
  • Management
  • Psychological drama

Your best friend is counting on you. Perhaps a little too much.

Everyday life isn’t always simple. Groceries, bills, surprises and small acts of care all demand time and money. When your best friend depends on you to move forward, every decision becomes a little more complicated.

🏡 An everyday life to organise

In Project CENTURY, you support someone who plays an important part in your life. Look after their needs, plan the days and balance different responsibilities. Some requests will be easy to meet. Others will require compromises.

💰 Every expense counts

Your budget isn’t endless. Manage your money, set priorities and decide how to meet everyday needs. A little purchase might bring joy today… but what about tomorrow?

💬 A changing relationship

Through conversations and events, discover a complex friendship shaped by attachment, habits and tension. Helping someone can be wonderful. Knowing where your responsibility ends isn’t always easy.

🌱 Finding a balance

Project CENTURY blends storytelling and management to explore human relationships, emotional dependence and personal boundaries.

Taking care of someone matters. Taking care of yourself matters too.

How much of yourself can you give?
